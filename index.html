Apex Oil Company Inc.

Corporate Profile

Founded in 1932, Apex Oil Company, Inc. has spent nearly a century at the forefront of the global energy landscape. As a premier wholesale distributor, we operate a sophisticated network dedicated to the storage, transport, and distribution of high-grade petroleum products including asphalt, diesel, and gasoline.

Based in St. Louis, Missouri, our operations are built on a foundation of logistical precision and market expertise. We bridge the gap between production and demand, ensuring a reliable supply of essential energy resources to industrial and municipal partners worldwide.
