Apex Oil Company Inc.

Company Overview

Apex Oil Company Inc. is a long-established participant in the United States petroleum sector, engaged in the wholesale distribution, storage, and transportation of refined petroleum products. Founded in 1932, the company has developed a reputation for operational consistency, logistical capability, and long-term industry presence.

With a primary focus on asphalt, diesel, and gasoline, Apex Oil supports commercial, industrial, and infrastructure-driven markets through reliable supply channels and integrated logistics solutions.

History & Foundation

Established in 1932, Apex Oil Company Inc. has evolved alongside the modern energy industry. Over decades of operation, the company has adapted to changes in market demand, regulatory frameworks, and transportation technologies while maintaining a focus on wholesale petroleum distribution.

Core Operations

Pipeline & Storage

The company supports petroleum storage and pipeline-linked distribution systems that enable efficient movement of refined products across regional markets.

Marine & Ground Transport

Apex Oil utilizes coordinated marine and ground transportation networks to facilitate bulk delivery of asphalt, diesel, and gasoline to wholesale customers.

Wholesale Distribution

Through structured supply agreements, the company distributes refined petroleum products to commercial buyers, infrastructure projects, and industrial partners.
