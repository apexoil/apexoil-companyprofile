Corporate Legacy

Founded in 1932, Apex Oil Company, Inc. has established nearly a century of dominance in the global energy markets. Headquartered in St. Louis, Missouri at 8235 Forsyth Blvd, Suite 900, we specialize in the wholesale distribution, storage, and transport of critical petroleum products including asphalt, diesel, and gasoline.

Our logistics network spans five continents, ensuring seamless energy supply chains through a commitment to operational excellence and market stability. We are more than a distributor; we are a global partner in energy security.

Strategic Refining

Our refining partnerships utilize high-complexity distillation to produce low-sulfur fuels that meet the highest international environmental standards.

Global Supply Chain

Utilizing a dedicated fleet of tankers and pipeline infrastructure, we maintain a 99.9% reliability rate in the transport of petroleum assets worldwide.

Market Intelligence

Our 24/7 trading desk provides price stability and risk management for wholesale partners, navigating the complexities of the global energy markets.
